# Property Investment Analysis

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of my Making Decisions with Data course at Utah State University.

The goal of the project was to use property data to help Aggie Investments identify promising real estate investment opportunities. The project was completed in multiple stages, beginning with exploratory data analysis and progressing into statistical modeling and clustering.

The focus of the project was to analyze the data, and to turn the results into information that could support a business decision.

## Business Problem

Aggie Investments needed to determine which types of properties could provide strong investment opportunities.

The analysis focused on understanding the characteristics of the available properties, identifying relationships between important variables, and finding groups of similar properties that could help guide an investment strategy.

The final goal was to use the results of the analysis to make a data-supported investment recommendation.

## Data

The dataset contained information about different properties and their characteristics.

Before completing the analysis, the data was explored and prepared so that the variables could be used for statistical analysis and modeling.

The project included:

- Exploring the distribution of important variables
- Identifying relationships between property characteristics
- Preparing variables for analysis
- Creating visualizations to better understand the data
- Using statistical methods to identify patterns

## Analysis

### Part 1: Exploratory Data Analysis

The first part of the project focused on understanding the dataset.

I used summary statistics and visualizations to explore the characteristics of the properties and identify patterns that could be useful later in the analysis.

This step helped determine which variables were most important to investigate further.

### Part 2: Statistical Analysis

The second part of the project used statistical methods to better understand relationships within the property data.

The goal was to move beyond basic visualizations and determine which variables could help explain differences between properties and potential investment opportunities.

This part of the project helped connect the exploratory analysis to a more data-driven investment decision.

### Part 3: Clustering & Recommendation

The final portion of the project used clustering to group properties with similar characteristics.

Instead of evaluating every property individually, clustering made it possible to identify different types of properties within the dataset.

These groups were then compared to determine which property segment appeared to provide the strongest opportunity for Aggie Investments.

## Key Findings

The analysis showed that properties could be separated into meaningful groups based on their characteristics.

Using multiple types of analysis provided a better understanding of the properties than relying on a single variable alone. Exploratory analysis helped identify patterns, statistical analysis helped examine relationships between variables, and clustering helped identify groups of similar investment opportunities.

The results were then used to support a final investment recommendation.

## Recommendation

Based on the results of the analysis, I identified the property group that best matched the investment goals of Aggie Investments.

Rather than making a recommendation based on one characteristic, the final decision considered multiple property characteristics and the patterns identified throughout the analysis.

This project demonstrated how data analysis can be used to turn a large dataset into a more manageable and informed business decision.
